Diffusion, dethroned?

A single step is all you need: ROMS-IMLE hits FID 2.56 on ImageNet without iterative denoising

For years the field assumed competitive generative modeling required many denoising steps. If this holds up, the sampling-cost advantage of diffusion stops being a law of nature and becomes an engineering choice — with big implications for latency-bound image and video systems.
