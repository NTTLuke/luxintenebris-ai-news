## Research & Papers

- **[GUI-HARVEST: Self-Improving GUI Agents Through Evidence-Driven Harness Evolution](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.00948)** — An automatic harness optimizer enabling self-improving GUI agents with frozen backbones, gaining 12.33 points on OSWorld-Verified with Qwen3-VL-32B. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[HuatuoGPT-3: RL-Only Domain Adaptation from Base Models](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.05966)** — OnePO one-stage policy optimization for RL-only medical domain adaptation, achieving 67.2 on HealthBench with 20K samples, surpassing SFT+RL baselines. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Knowing When Not to Answer: Cross-Domain Multi-Turn Generalization of Underspecification Signals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08413)** — LLMs routinely answer unanswerable questions; this work shows unanswerability is linearly decodable from hidden states and generalizes across domains and multi-turn dialogue. *(arXiv)*
- **[CoDe-LoRA: Mitigating the Orthogonality Dilemma in Continual Learning of LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08312)** — Reveals that orthogonal projection methods like O-LoRA create conflicting parameter updates in continual learning; proposes knowledge consolidation and decoupling to resolve catastrophic forgetting. *(arXiv)*
- **[Toward Alignment Scaling Laws: A Framework and First Preregistered Measurements](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08540)** — Treats alignment as a family of measurable scaling relations across risk categories, modeling the burden needed to hold a fixed safety target as models grow. *(arXiv)*
