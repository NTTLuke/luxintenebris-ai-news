## Research & Papers

- **[MoEMB scales universal multimodal embeddings with efficient Mixture-of-Experts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08663)** — MoE architecture grows multimodal embedding capacity without proportional compute, achieving strong retrieval across vision-language and image-text benchmarks while keeping inference efficient. *(arXiv)*
- **[Suan rectifies direct preference safety alignment in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08634)** — Rectified DPO formulation for safety alignment that addresses over-refusal and under-refusal trade-offs in open-weight models, claiming safety comparable to proprietary systems while preserving helpfulness. *(arXiv)*
- **[Difficulty-adaptive tree-structured policy expansion for RLVR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08650)** — Extends RLVR for large reasoning models with a tree-structured policy that adapts difficulty per branch, showing measurable gains in pass@k and single-sample accuracy over GRPO on math and code benchmarks. *(arXiv)*
- **[Environments as scaffold: enriching feedback to bootstrap self-evolving agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08404)** — Argues enriching environment-side feedback (rather than only warming up the agent) mitigates reward sparsity in long-horizon RL training, with ablations on agentic benchmarks showing faster convergence. *(arXiv)*
