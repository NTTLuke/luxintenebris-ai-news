## Research & Papers

- **[How to train a critic stably and efficiently for GRPO-based LLM post-training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23566)** — Proposes a stable critic training recipe showing that a well-trained critic can estimate token-level advantages from a single response matching GRPO's multi-sample performance. *(arXiv)*
- **[Reward-free continual adaptation for resilient space robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23452)** — Introduces a reward-free continual RL framework for space robots under hardware degradation, removing the need for reward signals during deployment while maintaining online adaptation. *(arXiv)*
- **[Credal LLMs for semantic commitment under uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23244)** — Credal LLMs maintain multiple predictive distributions to separate epistemic ignorance from aleatory ambiguity, improving calibration on ambiguous queries without sacrificing accuracy. *(arXiv)*
- **[SplitLite: low-rank residual compression for split learning on-device LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23018)** — Proposes a low-rank residual compression method reducing communication overhead in split learning while maintaining fine-tuning quality for on-device LLMs. *(arXiv)*
- **[A physical response-and-memory model for Muon optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22994)** — Derives a physically-motivated optimizer from a response-and-memory model, achieving competitive training loss against Muon and AdamW while providing theoretical grounding for update rules. *(arXiv)*
- **[Beyond the harness: end-to-end optimization of context artifacts for enterprise Text-to-SQL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22830)** — Shows that optimizing knowledge-base context artifacts (schema selection, table linking) outperforms model fine-tuning alone for enterprise Text-to-SQL. *(arXiv)*
- **[TailSieve: partial-rollout-guided tail routing for LLM rollouts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22788)** — A routing system that redirects tail-latency requests to faster surrogate models guided by partial rollout analysis, improving throughput in RL training and evaluation pipelines. *(arXiv)*
