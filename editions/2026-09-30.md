## Research & Papers

- **[Hidden reasoning must leak, but need not be readable: formal limits for chain-of-thought monitoring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37312)** — Asks whether reasoning models can perform hidden computation without revealing it in thinking traces. Shows the answer depends on task difficulty and model size: simple computations can be hidden from CoT monitors, with formal limits on monitorability. *(arXiv)*
- **[EasyPPO: stabilizing the critic is key to reliable LLM reinforcement learning](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.36802)** — Identifies the PPO critic as a major source of instability in RL for LLMs, pinpointing two critic failure modes. Stabilizing the critic is presented as the key to stable LLM RL training. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[S^3: spectral null-space swap makes reasoning models efficient](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37976)** — Finds that chain-of-thought capacity lives mainly in a thinking model's weight component within a null space defined relative to an Instruct model. A spectral null-space swap transfers reasoning ability while cutting excessive reasoning-token cost. *(arXiv)*
- **[LeapQuant: accurate recurrent-state quantization for linear-attention LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38166)** — Targets hybrid LLMs using linear attention (Gated DeltaNet, Kimi Delta Attention) and fixes the accuracy collapse that occurs when their fixed-size recurrent states are quantized to low precision, keeping long-context quality while cutting serving memory. *(arXiv)*
- **[SelfSearch: reward-free search for self-improving agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37968)** — Coding agents inspect and modify their own instructions, tools and execution procedures without any downstream reward signal, removing the repeated evaluation cost that existing self-improving agent loops incur. *(arXiv)*
