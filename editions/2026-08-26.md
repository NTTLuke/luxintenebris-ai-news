### AMD MI400 Helios rack architecture delivers 2.9 exaflops at Hot Chips 2026

AMD detailed the Instinct MI455X silicon with 40.26 peak petaflops (MXFP4), up to 4x the MI355X. Helios racks offer 2.9 exaflops, 31 TB of HBM4 memory, and 1.7 PB/s bandwidth across 72 GPUs paired with EPYC Venice CPUs.

## Research &amp; Papers

- **[On-policy self-distillation aligns diffusion models with human preferences](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.24646)** — DiffusionOPSD converts image-level rewards into fine-grained denoising supervision, bridging RL alignment with on-policy distillation for text-to-image models. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Meta$^n$: Recursive self-improvement through emergent depth in LLMs](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.24735)** — Self-improving agents typically cap out at two meta-levels. This paper proposes a mechanism that lets systems recursively edit their own editing machinery, unlocking deeper emergent reasoning. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[WeMM-Embedding: WeChat's universal multimodal embedding family](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.24053)** — A family of universal multimodal embeddings from Tencent, enabling heterogeneous content to share a representation space for retrieval, recommendation, classification, and agentic systems. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[NeuronGuard redistributes safety signals in LLMs against jailbreaks and pruning attacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23959)** — Both crafted jailbreak prompts and post-deployment neuron pruning exploit a common weakness: safety-relevant activations are concentrated in easily removed components. NeuronGuard redistributes them robustly. *(arXiv)*
- **[Contrastive Branch Policy Optimization improves sparse reward learning for tool agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24300)** — Branch sampling introduces local comparisons among intermediate decisions, addressing the sparse-outcome problem in multi-turn tool-calling RL for language models. *(arXiv)*
