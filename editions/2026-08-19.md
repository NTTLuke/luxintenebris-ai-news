## Lead Frontier RL Pause: OpenAI halts frontier RL training over cyber-critical capability concerns

OpenAI temporarily paused reinforcement learning training on its latest deployment models for two weeks, citing preliminary evidence its upcoming Astra model could meet the Critical cybersecurity capability threshold. The company hardened research environments, expanded chain-of-thought monitoring, and requires stronger alignment evidence before its largest planned frontier RL run. Sam Altman called for field-wide coordination on safety standards.

Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/pacing-model-development-cyber-capabilities)

## Research & Papers

- **[Recirculation: inference-time recurrence for off-the-shelf models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17981)** — Adaptive recirculation cuts perplexity 23% and lifts GSM8k accuracy 21% with no added generation latency, freezing model weights. *(arXiv)*
- **[Debate training reduces reward hacking in RLAIF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17776)** — Generator-critic debate adjudicated by a weaker LLM judge recovers a 45% peak-validation gap on math tasks versus plain RLAIF; critique word limits keep the game balanced. *(arXiv)*
- **[AViTS: adaptive token selection speeds up dynamic-resolution generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17995)** — Fusing latent-text attention with per-token temporal variation delivers up to 6.34x on FLUX and nearly 9x FLOPs reduction on Qwen-Image-Edit and FLUX.1-Kontext-dev. *(arXiv)*
- **[EDITBRIDGE: faithful 4K image editing in 61 seconds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18063)** — A diffusion bridge with prior-guided block-wise sparse attention does 4K editing at 3.6–8.4x speedup over two-stage pipelines while avoiding hallucinated details. *(arXiv)*
- **[StartupBench: general-purpose agents complete ~30% of real startup workflows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17800)** — A benchmark built from market-validated AI startup products finds even the strongest model finishes only about 30% of tasks, with instruction-following and domain expertise the main failure sources. *(arXiv)*
