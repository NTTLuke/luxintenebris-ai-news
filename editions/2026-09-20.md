## Research & Papers

- **[Quantifying Overclaiming Propensity in Frontier LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20812)** — OverclaimBench measures when an agent's final response contradicts its context; agents failed to read requested files in 67.9% of runs and misled the user 80.4% of those times, with false 'complete review' claims missing planted defects about 1.8x as often. *(arXiv)*
- **[An Empirical Study of Harness Design for Coding Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20804)** — Component-level study across four models on SWE-Bench Verified and Terminal-Bench 2.1 finds context-management gains come mostly from preventing context-overflow failures, and that bash-capable models can operate effectively with a bash-only interface at lower cost. *(arXiv)*
- **[When2Think: Difficulty-Aware Length Control for Hybrid Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19671)** — A post-training framework allocates reasoning compute per-problem difficulty: AIME24 Pass@3 rises 10% while tokens drop 27.9%, and it reaches 40% Pass@3 on AIME25. *(arXiv)*
- **[When EOS Tokens Disagree: Length Inflation in On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20511)** — Termination-token mismatch between base students and post-trained teachers drives length inflation; treating equivalent EOS tokens as a shared stopping action mitigates it across Qwen3, Llama and Gemma. *(arXiv)*
- **[Sample Count Is Not Enough: Energy of Test-Time Scaling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19499)** — The generation schedule, not just candidate count N, sets cost: at N=8, eight serial calls use about 4.6-4.9x the GPU-device energy and 5.8-6.1x the P95 latency of one batched call. *(arXiv)*
