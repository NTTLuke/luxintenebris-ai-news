## Lead Circular compute: Broadcom to lend Anthropic up to $42 billion to finance chip leases

Reporting surfaced in Anthropic's IPO prospectus says Broadcom has agreed to lend Anthropic up to $42 billion to finance its infrastructure spending, making the AI lab Broadcom's largest chip-design customer. The arrangement — layered on top of a reported $125.2 billion five-year TPU lease, with convertible financing covering about a third of it — sharpens concerns about circular financing among AI compute suppliers.

Source: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/broadcom-to-lend-anthropic-up-to-42-billion-in-deal-to-lease-chips-121617505.html)

## Research & Papers

- **[Sharpening Tax in Post-Training](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.01509)** — Tests whether RL post-training merely sharpens base-model behaviors — improving single-shot accuracy at the cost of solution coverage — and extends the analysis to agentic multi-turn tool use, where the trade-off need not hold. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Finetuning with Sampling: SFT Learns Better Than You Think](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02140)** — Challenges the view that SFT generalizes poorly versus RL, showing a sampling-based finetuning formulation substantially narrows the SFT-vs-RL generalization gap for new capabilities. *(arXiv)*
- **[On Language Drift during RLVR Post-Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02015)** — Documents how chains of thought drift toward increasingly non-standard, seemingly nonsensical phrasing as RLVR-trained capability rises, analyzing it as a side effect of post-training. *(arXiv)*
- **[Hierarchical Continuous Diffusion Language Models](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.02193)** — Targets the parallel-decoding bottleneck of diffusion language models with a hierarchical continuous-diffusion architecture that preserves inter-token dependencies for global constraint satisfaction. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Argo-Bench: Evaluating Data Agents on Enterprise-Scale Workflows](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.02122)** — A benchmark for data-science agents on enterprise workflows spanning dozens of tables, motivated by audits showing existing text-to-SQL benchmarks have frequently wrong answer keys. *(HuggingFace)*
