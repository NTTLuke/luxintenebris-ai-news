## Lead OPEN WEIGHTS: DeepSeek releases V4.1-Flash, a natively multimodal open-weights model

DeepSeek shipped V4.1-Flash with open weights on September 10 — a natively multimodal (vision and language) model built on a 552B sparse backbone with roughly 8B–16B active parameters. It supports up to 1M context, a shared compressed KV cache, a two-stage sparse indexer, and a 196B Engram lookup memory, with SGLang adding day-0 inference and reinforcement-learning support.

Source: [LMSYS / DeepSeek](https://x.com/lmsysorg/status/2097939906030919945)

## Research & Papers

- **[GPU-CFR: 80x faster counterfactual regret minimization by compiling the game to static dataflow and CUDA graph replay](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11923)** — Compiles CFR game-tree sweeps into static dataflow graphs with CUDA graph replay, delivering an 80x speedup over prior GPU implementations and, for the first time, making CFR run faster on GPUs than CPUs. *(arXiv)*
- **[SenseNova-U1.5: towards native unified visual intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11929)** — An 8B mixture-of-transformer multimodal model that understands, reasons about, and generates visual content in an encoder-free, VAE-free architecture, supporting native resolutions up to 4K via spatially coherent patch reconstruction. *(arXiv)*
- **[RetroThinker: enabling retrospective thinking in speech LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11864)** — Introduces a retrospective-thinking mechanism for speech-LLMs that recovers reasoning quality on complex tasks without the latency cost of full chain-of-thought, narrowing the reasoning gap to text-only models under real-time constraints. *(arXiv)*
- **[MindTopo: can foundation models reason in topological space?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11900)** — A benchmark evaluating topological (deformation-invariant) spatial reasoning across foundation models, showing they lean on metric and viewpoint-dependent relations and exposing systematic gaps in topological intuition. *(arXiv)*
- **[CausalArena: benchmarking causal discovery in the foundation model era](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11897)** — A standardized benchmark of structural causal models and evaluation protocols for causal discovery, targeting the emerging use of foundation models to uncover causal structure from data. *(arXiv)*
