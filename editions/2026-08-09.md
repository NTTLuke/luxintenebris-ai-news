## Lead Founding Exodus: Jeff Dean leaves Google after 27 years to found DiscoLoopAI

Jeff Dean, along with Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le, and Oriol Vinyals, departed Google to launch Discovery Loop (DiscoLoopAI), a startup focused on automating scientific discovery. Backed by Radical Ventures, Khosla Ventures, and Alphabet/Google itself.

### Prime Agent — open-source self-improving coding agent released under MIT license

Prime Intellect released Prime Agent, a fully open-source MIT-licensed coding agent with persistent autonomous workflows, subagent spawning, and a /refine mechanism. Works with any provider or 100% locally via Ollama.

### Yann LeCun: AGI requires additional conceptual breakthroughs beyond LLM scaling

LeCun argued that AGI needs more than pure LLM scaling — world models and new architectures are required. Compared his own 2018 move away from management at Meta to Demis Hassabis's recent role change at DeepMind.
