## Lead OpenAI's Biggest Drop: OpenAI releases GPT-5.6 Luna and Sol with 25x cost reduction, outperforming physicians in study

OpenAI unveiled its GPT-5.6 model family with three tiers — Sol (maximum power), Terra (balanced), and Luna (speed and credit-optimized). Luna at the lowest reasoning effort outperforms GPT-5.5 at its highest effort while costing 25x less. A blinded physician study found fewer flaws in GPT-5.6 responses than in physician-written ones, marking a major milestone in clinical AI. The company also teased a 'Super App' combining ChatGPT, Codex, and its AI assistant into a unified platform.

Source: [OpenAI](https://x.com/i/status/2075689779937833302)

## Top stories

### Humanoid robots successfully perform gallbladder surgery in first preclinical trial

UC San Diego researchers used teleoperated humanoid robots to perform gallbladder removal in a study published in Nature. Two humanoids worked side-by-side in a robot-robot team — the first proof-of-concept for humanoid robots in surgery.

Source: [The Robot Report](https://www.therobotreport.com/beyond-da-vinci-why-versatile-humanoid-robots-are-next-frontier-surgery/)

### AI² Robotics raises $735M at $3B valuation for wheeled humanoid robots

Shenzhen-based AI² Robotics raised roughly $735M from state-backed entities and corporate investors at a $2.8B valuation. Its wheeled humanoid AlphaBot 2, powered by the Alpha Brain VLA model, targets logistics, manufacturing, and biotech.

Source: [The Robot Report](https://www.therobotreport.com/ai%c2%b2-robotics-raises-735m-3b-valuation-wheeled-humanoid-robots/)

### Sam Altman says AI has been net job-creating so far, contrary to his own expectations

Altman stated AI has been net job-creating at current capability levels, pushing back against his earlier expectations of clearer displacement. He noted the trend could continue as the technology matures.

Source: [sama](https://x.com/sama/status/2076036901824532530)

## Research & Papers

- **[Vidu S1 achieves real-time interactive video generation at 42 FPS on consumer GPUs](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.03118)** — Vidu S1 uses TurboDiffusion and TurboServe to output 540p video at up to 42 FPS with voice-controlled digital character animation, infinite-length support, and no visual drift. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Jet-Long extends LLM context to 128K with zero-shot tuning-free RoPE method](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.07740)** — A tuning-free approach pairing a local RoPE-faithful window with dynamic long-range rescaling, achieving +4.79pp on RULER at 1.7B scale and best HELMET-RAG accuracy across Qwen3 models up to 128K context. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[UniClawBench offers first universal benchmark for proactive AI agents on real-world tasks](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.08768)** — A capability-driven benchmark with 400 bilingual real-world tasks evaluated in live Docker environments using step-by-step checkpoints and a closed-loop multi-agent evaluation setup. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Ideas Have Genomes benchmark reveals LLMs hit 27% accuracy on scientific lineage reasoning](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.08758)** — IdeaGene-Bench introduces 1,961 golden lineage traces across 10 domains. The strongest LLM-based scientist reaches only 27.3% exact accuracy, exposing a compositional bottleneck in AI scientific reasoning. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Flash-BoN speeds up diffusion model inference with cheap drafts and multi-stage verification](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.04461)** — Uses timestep truncation, layer skipping, and activation proxies to generate cheap draft candidates, achieving +8% AUC over baselines under fixed wall-clock budgets at larger model scales. *(HuggingFace)*
