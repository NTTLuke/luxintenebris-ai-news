## Research & Papers

- **[RRSI: Regularized recursive self-improvement of agent harnesses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24972)** — Because an agent's harness (prompts, control flow, tooling, memory) drives capability, this work regularizes recursive self-improvement of the harness itself, curbing memorization of training tasks while preserving gains. *(arXiv)*
- **[ARM: Attention with routed-memory for learnable sparse control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24417)** — A differentiable, fixed-size routed-memory KV cache learns which information to retain in long-context inference, beating selective eviction and pruning baselines by keeping core context in a learned sparse control path. *(arXiv)*
- **[KV-COBRA: KV cache compression via co-optimized bit-rank allocation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24298)** — The real limiter at extreme compression rates is how the budget is spread across heads; co-optimizing per-head low-rank truncation and scalar quantization dominates uniform allocation, with the biggest gains at low bit-rates. *(arXiv)*
- **[TTSE: a two-track online self-evolution framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24289)** — Agents evolve their own capabilities online in continuously interactive environments, treating environmental knowledge as living input rather than fixed data, and beat RL baselines that adapt to fixed task distributions. *(arXiv)*
- **[Fathom-Vaidya: medical reasoning with rubric-based rewards](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24480)** — Training LLMs on rubric-based rewards spanning diagnostic and multi-turn clinical reasoning improves performance on HealthBench and MedXpert relative to standard reward schemes. *(arXiv)*
