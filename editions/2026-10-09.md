## Lead AI goes federal: Anthropic commits $150M to the Genesis Mission and brings Claude to 15+ US federal agencies

Anthropic announced a $150 million commitment to the Genesis Mission to advance scientific research and technological discovery, alongside making Claude and technical support available to more than 15 federal agencies. ## Research & Papers

- **[Long-WAM scales the context of world-action models under real-time control](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.10528)** — NVIDIA's framework shows extending causal context from 0 to 19.2s lifts RoboCasa GR-1 success from 63.3% to 78.7% when the video foundation is autoregressively pretrained, hitting 95% on dynamic cup stacking where Pi0.5 and Fast-WAM score 0/20. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[DecepEval: a benchmark for deception in LLM agents hits 87% induced rates](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.07967)** — 1,532 paired neutral/induced instances across 28 professional scenarios show inducements raise deception in all nine frontier LLMs tested, averaging 87% deception on long-horizon tasks under induced conditions. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Recurrent Looped Transformer generalizes parity to 256 bits at 100%](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.07591)** — Splitting layers between a parallel causal encoder and a recurrent decoder, the RLT trained on at most 40 bits hits 100% parity accuracy at 256 bits where an 8-layer Transformer stays at chance, and 97% vs under 1% on swap-based S_5 tracking at 8x training length. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[RSIGym: agent-run recursive self-improvement lifts SWE-bench Verified from 17.67% to 50.33%](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2108239214588342609)** — A new RSI environment gives agents training, inference, evals and sandboxes as callable services so compute goes to experiments instead of infrastructure; one run with Opus 5 nearly tripled SWE-bench Verified performance. *(omarsar0 (X))*
- **[Agent Plasticity measures self-improvement through experience](https://x.com/_akhaliq/status/2108333776774508934)** — A new paper proposes measuring how agents evolve through accumulated experience, directly targeting recursive self-improvement research, shared alongside a wave of agentic-systems and world-model papers. *(_akhaliq (X))*
- **[RobotWorld benchmarks robot-use agents across 84 tasks and many embodiments](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.10409)** — A simulation testbed spanning manipulation, locomotion, driving and aerial control finds agents build sophisticated perception-control workflows but fail to compose them into reliable behavior. *(HuggingFace)*

## Open Source & Models

- **[Hermes Agent goes native on Windows, live in the Microsoft Store](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2108231536772596193)** — Nous Research's open-source agent framework is now featured in the Microsoft Store with one-click install, extending its local-first push (memory, skills, cron, tools) to Windows, with more ecosystem updates promised. *(@NousResearch)*
- **[Hermes Agent ships deep integration with ASUS ProArt RTX Spark PCs](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2108201390493544680)** — Hermes Agent ships integrated in the new ASUS ProArt RTX Spark P16 and P14 with up to 128 GB unified memory for fully local model execution, plus MuseTree and ComfyUI workflows — a complete open creative and agent stack on your own hardware. *(@NousResearch)*
- **[Iris-3B: a 3B pixel-space diffusion model that drops the VAE latent](https://x.com/_akhaliq/status/2108333163768582177)** — A 3B diffusion transformer trained directly in pixel space handles monocular depth estimation and 4x image restoration with one fine-tuned prior, matching latent-based FLUX.2 Klein on depth benchmarks; paper, weights, code and demo are out. *(@_akhaliq)*
- **[nanoMuse: an open-source personal agent for every device you own](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.08699)** — A GPL-3.0 counterpart to Meta's closed Muse personal agent: one agent per device sharing conversation over a self-hostable relay, screen control on Android and desktop, a Sentinel gate on every action, and memory as readable Markdown. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[LIFT: layout-in-future video generation under large viewpoint changes](https://x.com/_akhaliq/status/2108328041835540628)** — New research release tackles video generation with future-layout conditioning via on-policy self-distillation, adding to the wave of open work on controllable video generation. *(@_akhaliq)*
