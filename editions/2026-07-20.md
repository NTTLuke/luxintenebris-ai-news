## Research & Papers

- **[Xiaomi-Robotics-1: Scaling VLA models with 100K hours of real-world trajectories](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.15330)** — A foundational vision-language-action model trained on over 100,000 hours of real-world manipulation data achieves SOTA 57.6% on RoboCasa365 (beating prior 46.6%) and 20.07 on RoboDojo (beating prior 13.07). *(HuggingFace)*
- **[DSWorld: A data science world model for efficient autonomous agents](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.15901)** — A world model that predicts data science operation effects before execution, combining structured states, cost-aware routing, and an LLM simulator to accelerate RL-based agent training by ~14x and search-based inference by 3-6x. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[From Pixels to States: Interactive world models as game engines](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.14076)** — Proposes using video generative models as interactive game engines that respond coherently to player actions from pixels, demonstrating data-driven world simulation without explicit 3D rendering. 407 upvotes on HuggingFace. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Audio-Visual Flamingo: Open AV-LLM for long and complex videos](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.16107)** — An open audio-visual LLM for joint understanding of audio, images, and long-form video, supported by a ~7M instance Audio-Visual-Skills dataset and three-stage curriculum training. Outperforms similar-sized open models on 15+ benchmarks. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Qwen-Music Technical Report: High-fidelity music generation from Alibaba](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.11699)** — Qwen-Music produces high-fidelity songs with complete vocal singing, supporting text-to-music and reference-based music generation with strong musicality. *(HuggingFace)*
