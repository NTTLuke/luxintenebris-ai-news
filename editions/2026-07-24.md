## Lead AMD's Big AI Bet: AMD Launches Helios Rack-Scale AI System With MI455X GPU and 6th Gen EPYC Venice, Signs OpenAI and Anthropic as Customers

At Advancing AI 2026, AMD unveiled Helios — a rack-scale AI solution packing 72 MI455X GPUs (2nm, 432GB HBM4 each) and 18 EPYC Venice CPUs rated at 2.9 exaflops FP4 inference. OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, Microsoft, and Oracle are confirmed customers. AMD separately announced a strategic partnership with Anthropic to deploy up to 2GW of MI450-series GPUs and a $5B equity investment.

Source: [AMD](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1294/aai-2026-amd-delivers-full-stack-compute-for-the-agentic-ai-era)
