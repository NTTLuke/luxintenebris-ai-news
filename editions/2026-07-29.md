## Research & Papers

- **[IMACS: Decoupling organization, coordination, and collaboration in multi-agent LLM systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25446)** — Multi-agent frameworks routinely entangle three logically distinct concerns: who is on the team, how members align, and which algorithm fuses their work. IMACS introduces a decoupled taxonomy evaluated across 10 benchmark environments. *(arXiv)*
- **[Retraction-free optimization over the Stiefel manifold for LoRA fine-tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25299)** — A retraction-free optimizer avoids costly orthonormalization during LoRA fine-tuning, achieving 2-3x speedup over standard methods while maintaining convergence quality on LLM tasks. *(arXiv)*
- **[CLBench-V: Evaluating multimodal context learning from grounding to knowledge acquisition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25294)** — A multimodal benchmark reveals that even top MLLMs fail at basic grounding tasks (color, shape, counting) while excelling at knowledge transfer — exposing a gap in atomic visual perception. *(arXiv)*
- **[Shieldstral: A 3B-parameter multimodal safety classifier matching models 7x its size](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.25857)** — A policy-adaptive multimodal safety classifier that matches or outperforms models nearly 7x its size on text safety benchmarks and sets a new SOTA on multimodal safety classification. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Raven: High-recall sequence modeling with sparse memory routing at 32K tokens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25357)** — A linear-time sequence model with sparse memory routing that achieves near-perfect recall on long-range dependency tasks up to 32K tokens, outperforming SSMs and linear Transformers on the Long Range Arena. *(arXiv)*
