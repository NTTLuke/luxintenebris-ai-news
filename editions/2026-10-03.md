## Lead Automated discovery: Google Research Cogentic produces new proofs on five open problems in theoretical computer science

Cogentic, a multi-agent system from Google Research, started from bare problem statements with no expert hints and produced five new verified results on open problems in online learning, auction theory and mechanism design. The system runs orchestrator, prover, summarizer, adversarial verifier, auditor and process-advisor agents over iterative rounds — a notable step for automated proof discovery in theory, not just applied ML.

Source: [Google Research via X (@omarsar0)](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2106056369816420624)

## Research & Papers

- **[Where-OPD: spatially guided on-policy self-distillation of MLLMs with synthetic scenes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02117)** — Trains a multimodal student against an EMA teacher that receives privileged spatial information, improving spatial reasoning. *(arXiv)*
- **[CARM: cancellation-aware response masking for LLM reinforcement learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02039)** — Filters cancelled or failed rollouts in RL post-training, improving training stability and benchmark results on math and code. *(arXiv)*
- **[Asynchronous LLM post-training: group-mass capping and convergence analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01896)** — Gives theoretical guarantees for asynchronous RL post-training with stale rollouts, proposing group-mass capping as a correction. *(arXiv)*
- **[Feature selective model collapse in diffusion models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01318)** — Shows collapse from training on synthetic data is feature-selective: some semantic features degrade while others persist, depending on the training regime. *(arXiv)*
- **[Scaling and distilling text embeddings for better diffusibility](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.01016)** — Finds that scaling the embedding model improves the latent-space 'diffusibility' for continuous diffusion language models, and proposes distilled embeddings optimized for it. *(Hugging Face)*
