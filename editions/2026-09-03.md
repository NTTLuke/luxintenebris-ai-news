## Research & Papers

- **[Post-training language models achieve gold-medal performance in coding competitions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02849)** — An end-to-end pipeline combining large-scale problem curation, synthetic reasoning traces, and super-specialization achieves gold-medal performance at IOI/ICPC level. *(arXiv)*
- **[Discriminative world models outperform generative prediction for web agent planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02885)** — Proposes discriminative world models that rank predicted web states from candidate actions, outperforming generative next-state prediction for test-time web agent planning. *(arXiv)*
- **[From reweighting to rewriting: unlocking intervention effects of influential training samples](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02771)** — Shows rewriting — not just reweighting — is needed for effective training data attribution; the proposed method outperforms influence functions on intervention tasks. *(arXiv)*
- **[Dutch books for language models: LLM probability forecasts violate coherence axioms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02797)** — Shows that LLMs' probabilistic forecasts for life decisions violate coherence axioms (Dutch books), undermining trust in their world model consistency. *(arXiv)*
- **[ByteDance Seed publishes research on self-evolving agent harnesses that score on harness quality](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2095170896407548190)** — ByteDance Seed published a paper on self-evolving agent harnesses that score on harness quality rather than just task completion, matching or exceeding human baselines in writing and ML experimentation. *(omarsar0)*
