## Research & Papers

- **[NCP-ArchPreview: latent space language models through next concept prediction](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.10715)** — Scales a latent-space LM to 8.9B parameters on 5.73T tokens, matching OLMo-3-7B's final pretraining loss with 51.3% of the tokens and beating it by 2.45 points on downstream macro-average. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[An open recipe for IMO gold: training Nemotron for olympiad mathematics](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.10712)** — A natural-language test-time-compute pipeline built on three Nemotron 3 Ultra checkpoints scored 30/42 at IMO 2026, reaching the gold-medal threshold with no formal prover, tools or internet. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[Negative self-distillation: learning to reason by avoiding flaws](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.11699)** — Diverges the student from a self-generated 'careless reasoner' teacher using dynamic gating that isolates reasoning-critical tokens, outperforming on-policy self-distillation and label-free RL baselines. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[Memory as plans: world-action modeling with memory-grounded planning](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.11561)** — Represents memory as segment plans for non-Markovian manipulation, hitting state-of-the-art on RMBench with 83.3% success and 78.0% on real-robot tasks at constant inference latency. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[FreeFlow: a bias-free hierarchical transformer for optical flow estimation](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.11486)** — A transformer with no flow-specific inductive biases reaches state-of-the-art on Sintel, KITTI-2015 and Spring while staying memory-efficient at 1080p. *(Hugging Face)*

## Hardware & Robotics

- **[Figure 03 becomes the closely watched test case for physical AI](https://www.humanoid.press/humanoid-daily/)** — A deep report details Figure's third-generation humanoid, its Helix 02 vision-language-action model, a 1-robot-per-hour BotQ rate past 1,000 units, and its paid BMW logistics deployment. *(Humanoid Press)*
- **[AgiBot's GE-Act 2.0 trains a world-action model from scratch and lifts zero-shot success from 17.1% to 44.1%](https://www.humanoid.press/humanoid-daily/)** — GE-Act 2.0 is a world-action model trained from random initialization on embodied manipulation data, running a 104 ms plan loop on one RTX 5090 as co-training data scaled from 300 to 30,000 hours. *(Humanoid Press)*
- **[DEEP Robotics takes the Lynx wheeled-legged robot into the Tengger Desert for desertification control](https://www.humanoid.press/humanoid-daily/)** — The Lynx M20S patrols sand-control work in the desert carrying a ~35 kg payload near 50 C with IP67 dust sealing, a dual-light inspection rig and a loudspeaker to deter grazing animals. *(Humanoid Press)*
