## Lead Agents in your IDE: Google brings Antigravity coding agents to any IDE with new extensions

Google launched Antigravity IDE Extensions, putting its coding agents inside existing editors so developers can launch and monitor several agents at once without leaving their IDE. The move turns any editor into an agent host, a step toward the supervised multi-agent workflow many teams are converging on.

Source: [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/google-antigravity)

## Top stories

### Structured conflict beats naive agent scaling in multi-agent code review

Adding more coding agents hits diminishing returns on repository-level tasks. A three-agent setup (coder, reviewer, critic) with encouraged, evidence-based disagreement beats a 5-agent baseline on LiveCodeBench and posts the highest F1 on SWE-PRBench.

Source: [Dair.ai (@omarsar0)](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2091631620025647184)

### LeCun pushes research beyond LLMs toward human-efficient real-world learning

Yann LeCun again urged students to focus on architectures that learn physical, real-world tasks as efficiently as humans and animals, and pushed back on overhyped AGI-is-imminent narratives.

Source: [Yann LeCun (@ylecun)](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2091596538099196076)
