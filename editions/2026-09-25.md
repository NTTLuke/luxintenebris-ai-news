## Research & Papers

- **[GRASP: a multi-stage LLM planning framework for strategic agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30147)** — A strategy-aware multi-stage planning framework using LLMs that generates, revises, and assesses executable plans for complex tasks. *(arXiv)*
- **[Rufus-Air: open 8-stage post-training recipe for 106B MoE models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29421)** — Reproducible 8-stage post-training pipeline (SFT, reasoning/coding/agent RL, RLHF) on GLM-4.5-Air-Base 106B MoE, making advanced post-training fully open-source. *(arXiv)*
- **[Beyond Average Safety: chance-constrained fine-tuning bounds per-prompt risk](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29960)** — Introduces chance-constrained fine-tuning that bounds safety regression risk on a per-prompt basis rather than controlling average safety loss. *(arXiv)*
- **[SLCA-GRPO: fixing credit misattribution in tool-calling RL agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29050)** — Fixes GRPO credit assignment in tool-calling agents by segment-level advantage computation, preventing structural misattribution across heterogeneous outputs. *(arXiv)*
- **[Forecast-Dojo: replayable benchmark for training and evaluating LLM forecasting agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28876)** — Combines resolved prediction-market questions with dated news to create replayable environments for LLM-based forecasting agents. *(arXiv)*
- **[IterSynth: role-decoupled deep search agents outperform ReAct-style architectures](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.29444)** — Separates planning, evidence retrieval, and synthesis into distinct modules, outperforming ReAct-style agents on deep search tasks. *(HuggingFace)*
