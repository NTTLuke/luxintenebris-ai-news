## Research & Papers

- **[SpeakerMem-R1: speaker-centered dual-track memory for multi-party dialogue](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26780)** — Long-term conversational memory that distinguishes who said what, whom each statement concerns, how individuals perceive one another, and how group-shared information changes over time. *(arXiv)*
- **[PACT: from credit assignment to critic alignment for RL in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26355)** — Formalizes three regularity conditions (completeness, additivity, policy-consistency) bridging token-level credit assignment and critic alignment in reinforcement learning for language models. *(arXiv)*
- **[You only need 2/3 of the chosen experts: dynamic expert pruning in fine-grained MoE LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25809)** — Dynamic expert pruning achieves 33% throughput improvement with negligible quality drop, validated across fine-grained open-weight MoE architectures that routinely select many experts per token. *(arXiv)*
- **[Toolcompass: guiding tool trialing, not suppressing it, for LLM agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25678)** — Addresses the tool-trialing budget problem in LLM agents that must generalize to unseen tools at deployment, using outcome-based post-training guidance instead of brute suppression. *(arXiv)*
- **[Ladders of Thought: self-evolving curriculum of simplified reasoning traces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25643)** — Improves small- and mid-scale LLM reasoning by combining progressive question simplification with self-evolving reasoning traces, addressing brittleness that persists even with knowledge distillation. *(arXiv)*
- **[The Sirens' Song: when proximal background context overshadows distant evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26718)** — Identifies the Proximity Trap in long-context LLMs: insufficient attention to distant evidence arises less from distance than from cumulative competing background context. *(arXiv)*
- **[Train where the quantized model goes: on-policy distillation for low-bit reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26708)** — Proposes on-policy distillation to address repetitive degenerate loops in long generations that plague quantization-aware distillation below 3-bit precision. *(arXiv)*
