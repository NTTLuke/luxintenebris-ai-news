## Research & Papers

- **[Learning when to trust via selective context preference optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06377)** — SCOPE trains models to selectively trust external context via preference optimization, achieving robust accuracy improvements on context-dependent QA benchmarks without ignoring useful signals. *(arXiv)*
- **[AgentOPSD: recursive self-distillation for agentic reinforcement learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05987)** — Addresses credit assignment in long-horizon multi-turn agentic tasks via recursive self-distillation of privileged trajectory-level advantage estimates, outperforming GRPO on agentic benchmarks. *(arXiv)*
- **[GROM: gradient-free rapid one-shot machine unlearning for LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05783)** — One-shot machine unlearning method for LLMs that operates without gradient computation, enabling rapid removal of specific knowledge at a fraction of the computational cost of fine-tuning approaches. *(arXiv)*
- **[EcoAgent-Bench: evaluating economic decision-making in budget-constrained LLM agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05519)** — Benchmark where agents must choose between resources of varying cost — local lookup, search, composite tools, model upgrades — testing economic reasoning under realistic budget constraints. *(arXiv)*
- **[APQF: agentic profiling-guided structured pruning and mixed-precision quantization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05499)** — Automated compression framework combining structured pruning and mixed-precision quantization guided by agentic profiling, removing manual expert choices across diverse architectures. *(arXiv)*
