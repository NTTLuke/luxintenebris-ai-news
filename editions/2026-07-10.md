## Research & Papers

- **[Gemma 4 Technical Report: Google's open-weight multimodal LLMs set new SOTA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02770)** — Google introduces open-weight multimodal LLMs (2.3B-31B, dense + MoE) with thinking mode, encoder-free 12B architecture, and KV-cache optimizations, establishing SOTA on STEM, multimodal, and long-context benchmarks. *(arXiv)*
- **[Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion: NVIDIA unifies autoregressive, diffusion and self-speculation decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05722)** — NVIDIA's tri-mode LM at 8B scale decodes 6x more tokens per forward than Qwen3-8B with comparable accuracy, delivering 4x higher throughput on SPEED-Bench. *(arXiv)*
- **[SenseNova-Vision: All CV tasks reformulated as unified multimodal generation](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.06560)** — Formulates detection, segmentation, depth, keypoints and pose as multimodal generation within a single UMM without task-specific heads, matching specialized systems across diverse vision benchmarks. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[RynnWorld-4D: Alibaba's 4D embodied world model for robotic manipulation](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.06559)** — Jointly predicts synchronized RGB, depth and optical flow from a single RGB-D image and language instruction. Features a tri-branch Diffusion Transformer enabling >9 Hz closed-loop bimanual control. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Flex-Forcing: NVIDIA unifies autoregressive and bidirectional video diffusion](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.03509)** — A unified video diffusion framework enabling flexible generation with both forward and bidirectional conditioning in a single architecture. *(HuggingFace)*
