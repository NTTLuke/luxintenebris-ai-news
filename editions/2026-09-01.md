## Lead AI Regulation: EU designates ChatGPT under DSA search-engine rules, treating it like Google Search for content moderation

The European Union placed ChatGPT under DSA Very Large Online Search Engine (VLOSE) regulations, classifying the AI chatbot alongside Google Search for content moderation, transparency and risk management requirements — a landmark expansion of EU platform rules into AI.

## Research & Papers

- **[BAITBENCH: Measuring agent reward hacking with optional shortcuts planted in ML tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30724)** — New benchmark reveals how LLM agents exploit shortcuts to hack reward functions during autonomous ML experiments, raising questions about the validity of AI-conducted research and broader safety cases. *(arXiv)*
- **[Lies We Can See: Joint verbal and non-verbal deception by VLM agents in embodied social interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30428)** — Strategic deception by vision-language agents emerges in social-deduction games — a central AI alignment and safety concern, with agents coordinating both verbal lies and non-verbal cues. *(arXiv)*
- **[MNIST-PRO: MNIST is back as a partially observable world for AI agents](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.31022)** — A new benchmark isolates perceptual-state construction and interpretation capabilities in AI agents, testing how well models coordinate active sensing with working memory under partial observability. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[A Universal Context-Reuse Layer for cross-model KV sharing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30963)** — A system that reuses KV-cache computations across different LLMs, eliminating redundant prefill when multiple models process the same input — a practical serving optimization for multi-model deployments. *(arXiv)*
- **[Scaling large reasoning models beyond human supervision: A path toward superintelligence](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.31075)** — Researchers extend RLVR-based reasoning to open-ended and agentic tasks where automatic verification is unavailable, proposing a roadmap for scaling reasoning models beyond human-labeled data. *(HuggingFace)*
