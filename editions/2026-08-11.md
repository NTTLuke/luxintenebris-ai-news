## Lead Frontier cyber defense: OpenAI launches GPT-5.6-Cyber as it expands Daybreak into two security tiers

OpenAI unveiled GPT-5.6-Cyber, a frontier cyber model built on GPT-5.6 Sol and reserved for the new Daybreak Red tier serving trusted partners like Accenture, IBM, CrowdStrike and Cloudflare. The expanded Daybreak program now splits into Blue for basic defense work and Red for security testing and vulnerability research, with OpenAI reporting the model surfaced previously unknown flaws including in Chrome's V8 engine.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/10/as-ai-led-attacks-multiply-openai-launches-a-new-cyber-model/)

## Research & Papers

- **[Anthropic research Claude pushes Riemann hypothesis lower bound to 67.2%](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2086867246073401655)** — An unreleased research version of Claude raised the proven lower bound on the fraction of Riemann zeta zeros satisfying the hypothesis from 41.6% to 67.2% — real progress on a long-standing open problem, without solving it. *(Anthropic)*
- **[Stealing reasoning traces from proprietary LLM APIs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09867)** — A new work demonstrates a practical attack that recovers hidden chain-of-thought from encrypted client-side blocks, exposing an information-leakage vector against encrypted CoT schemes. *(arXiv)*
- **[Model diffing for feature discovery and control in multimodal LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09928)** — A model-diffing approach decomposes MLLM hidden states into interpretable feature directions, enabling feature discovery, auditing, and post-hoc control of visual-language behavior. *(arXiv)*
- **[SKALD distills skills into weights, not prompts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09826)** — An on-policy self-distillation framework distills abstract skills into model weights for cases where verifiable-reward RL yields no group-relative signal — 63-68% of groups in their experiments. *(arXiv)*
- **[MixFormer: a linear transformer with mixture of memory experts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09468)** — A linear-transformer variant adds input adaptivity and larger memory capacity via a mixture-of-memory-experts design, targeting information loss in SSM-style long-context modeling. *(arXiv)*
