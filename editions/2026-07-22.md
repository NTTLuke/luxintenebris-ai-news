## Research & Papers

- **[ROMS-IMLE proves iterative denoising is not essential for competitive generative modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19332)** — A minimalist approach combining IMLE training with a moderate convolutional network achieves FID 2.56 on ImageNet 256 in a single step with strong precision and recall, challenging the necessity of the iterative denoising paradigm. *(arXiv)*
- **[Mage-Flow: 4B-parameter native-resolution image generation in 0.59 seconds on a single A100](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19064)** — A compact generative stack with a lightweight Mage-VAE tokenizer and native-resolution rectified flow DiT generates 1024² images in 0.59s and edits in 1.02s, with 2.5x training throughput improvement. *(arXiv)*
- **[GEAR: Evidence-aware RL rewards improve long-context reasoning by +4.6 points](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19345)** — A reward shaping method augments accuracy with grounding reward for key evidence and distractor penalty for irrelevant context, achieving consistent improvements across multiple model scales with larger gains at longer contexts. *(arXiv)*
- **[AdaFlash: Adaptive diffusion drafters boost speculative decoding throughput by 66%](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19223)** — Addresses variance in diffusion drafters with on-policy distillation and an adaptive length head, achieving up to 66% higher throughput than prior state-of-the-art in high-concurrency scenarios. *(arXiv)*
- **[AlayaWorld: a 15B video diffusion transformer for interactive long-horizon world modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18367)** — Full technical report of a model generating 24-fps video at 540p/720p autoregressively with persistent sink frame, compressed temporal history, and geometry-aligned spatial memory, reducing inference from ~30 to 4 steps per chunk. *(arXiv)*
