## Research & Papers

- **[ComPO: a zeroth-order paradigm for LLM preference alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19144)** — A comparison-oracle method that extracts directional information from preference pairs without optimizing a differentiable preference loss, with a convergence guarantee for its offline scheme. *(arXiv)*
- **[PANORAMA: panoptic grounded captioning via mask proposal selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19143)** — Introduces the PanoCaps dataset and the panoptic grounded captioning task, requiring VLMs to describe both foreground objects and background regions with pixel-level grounding. *(arXiv)*
- **[ScienceIDE: turning the world's scientific codebase into agent-learnable environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19134)** — Converts scientific code into programmable environments for agents, guided by expert-defined cases and acceptance criteria to ease the 'scientific experience bottleneck'. *(arXiv)*
- **[Higher-order pruning of experts in mixture-of-experts language models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18916)** — HOPE is a second-order expert-pruning objective that provably minimizes an upper bound on pruning error, beating REAP across three frontier MoE models. *(arXiv)*
- **[Infinite-parameter LLMs: generating and adapting weights from live data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18842)** — A deployed MoE model learns from current user-provided facts and corrections instead of relying only on static pretraining, by generating weights from live interaction data. *(arXiv)*
