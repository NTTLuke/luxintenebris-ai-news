## Lead Gateway Acquired: Stripe will reportedly acquire AI gateway startup OpenRouter for $7B+

Stripe has finalized a deal to acquire OpenRouter, the AI model gateway startup, for more than $7 billion, per Bloomberg. OpenRouter, which raised a $113M Series B at a $1.3B valuation in May 2026, claims 8 million users and access to 400+ models. The deal marks one of the largest AI infrastructure acquisitions to date, consolidating API access to the fragmented model ecosystem under Stripe's payments infrastructure.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/16/stripe-will-reportedly-acquire-ai-gateway-startup-openrouter-for-7b/)

### Nous Research launches Hermes Agent Desktop App

Nous Research released a cross-platform desktop app for Hermes Agent on macOS, Windows, and Linux, with 19x faster session loading, free models, support for local/open-source models, and a Blank Slate Mode for zero-configuration starting points.

Source: [NousResearch](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2089055111125307813)

### Yann LeCun publishes major defense of open foundation models

LeCun posted a detailed argument that AI progress depends on widely shared open foundation models, comparing the technology to the printing press and the internet, and arguing that competition over centralization is the right risk mitigation strategy.

Source: [ylecun](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2088880284129210405)
