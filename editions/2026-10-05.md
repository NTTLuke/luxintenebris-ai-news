## Lead Chip Diplomacy: Huawei and Qualcomm sign broad patent cross-license covering AI and chip technologies

Qualcomm agreed to license patents behind Huawei's LogicFolding chipmaking technique in a multi-year cross-license deal spanning 5G, AI, compute, and networking. Qualcomm also purchased certain Huawei US patents in compute, AI, and networking, marking a significant thaw in US-China chip industry collaboration.

Source: [Huawei](https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2026/10/qualcomm-broad-patent-agreement)

## Top stories

### LeCun argues distillation economics will make frontier models free or open

Yann LeCun posted that training frontier models is extremely expensive but distilling them into capable small models is cheap, creating market forces that push foundation models toward free or open distribution.

Source: [@ylecun](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2106783662117151003)

### China spent over $13 billion stockpiling ASML DUV lithography systems for AI chip production

China accumulated an estimated 343 immersion DUV lithography systems by early 2026, spending over $13 billion on about 90 systems in 2024 and another 89 in 2025. The stockpile can support 7nm-class logic and AI processor production including Huawei's Ascend series.

Source: [TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/10/05/news-china-reportedly-spent-13b-stockpiling-asml-duv-tools-in-2024-25-as-advanced-chip-push-continues/)

### Karpathy: more than 99% of people engaging with AI arrived in the last year

Andrej Karpathy notes the massive onboarding wave — the vast majority of AI participants arrived in the last year, creating friction and confusion for pre-2026 veterans.

Source: [@karpathy](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2106806571321966793)
