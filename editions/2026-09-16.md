## Research & Papers

- **[JustFit: how to serve 200K-token LLM contexts on a 24 GiB laptop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17475)** — An MLX runtime combining compressed KV execution, component residency and state preservation keeps big contexts usable on laptop memory. *(arXiv)*
- **[When should LLMs abstain? Chain-of-Self-Questioning for selective risk control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17516)** — A prompt-only framework conditions answer commitment on an explicit check of required information, improving abstention accuracy with no fine-tuning. *(arXiv)*
- **[Mo' Models, Mo' Problems: selecting model pools for multi-agent systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17306)** — Evaluating eight model-selection strategies shows how pool composition affects reasoning performance across a growing field of open models. *(arXiv)*
- **[Where should a document live: context, parameters, or latent representations?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17346)** — Comparing three ways to inject new knowledge into LLMs, and their trade-offs on questions outside the training distribution. *(arXiv)*
- **[Coupled calibration and learning: fixing teacher bias in LLM distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17474)** — A method that keeps distillation from transferring a teacher's systematic bias, under covariate shift and without target-domain reward feedback. *(arXiv)*
