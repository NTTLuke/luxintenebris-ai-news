## Lead Chip geopolitics: China may allow ByteDance and Alibaba to buy Nvidia's RTX Pro 5500 chips

China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology signaled it may let Alibaba and ByteDance purchase Nvidia's RTX Pro 5500 Blackwell workstation GPU (84 GB GDDR7, 21,760 CUDA cores, >$6,000). ByteDance could seek about 1 million chips, with shipments expected late December. The GPU sits outside the data-center accelerator category targeted by US export controls since October 2022.

Source: [Digg](https://digg.com/world-business/63fbqtnr)

## Top stories

### Panda launches the world's first personal AI computer

Panda is a local AI computer that runs entirely in-home with no cloud or subscription, handling chat, code, research, image/video/3D generation for up to 25 simultaneous users. Preorder at $2,997, shipping December 2027.

Source: [Hacker News](https://pandax1.com)

### MiniMax launches M3.1-Flash-Preview with 5-tier reasoning slider

MiniMax's new coding model lets developers allocate test-time compute per task from sub-second completions to 45-second deep architectural verification via a five-tier reasoning slider (low/med/high/xhigh/max).

Source: [MiniMax](https://x.com/MiniMax_AI/status/2104256406786547800)

### Jev catches alignment failures at a fraction of LLM judge cost

TypeSafe AI's Jev uses a single yes/no probability question to detect sycophancy, jailbreaks, deception, prompt injection, and reward hacking with median AUROC ~0.886 at $0.30 per evaluation vs $19 for LLM judges.

Source: [omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2104295014117589053)

### LeCun warns domestic robot demos repeat self-driving hype cycle

LeCun compares current claims of domestic robots to 2016 self-driving demos, noting a decade later Level 5 autonomy remains unsolved and cautioning against overinterpreting short AI robotics demos.

Source: [ylecun](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2104232632058085593)
