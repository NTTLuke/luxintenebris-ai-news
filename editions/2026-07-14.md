## Research & Papers

- **[Video generation models are general-purpose vision learners (GenCeption)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09024)** — Uses a pre-trained video generative diffusion backbone as a feed-forward perception model. Achieves SOTA on depth, surface normal, camera pose, and 3D keypoint prediction with 7-500x less training data than prior methods. *(arXiv)*
- **[Long-Horizon-Terminal-Bench: Testing limits of agents on long-horizon terminal tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08964)** — Introduces 46 long-horizon terminal tasks with dense reward grading. The strongest frontier model achieves just 15.2% pass@1, with agents consuming avg 9.9M tokens per task. *(arXiv)*
- **[GATS: Graph-augmented tree search with layered world models for agent planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08894)** — Combines UCB1-based tree search with a 3-layer world model, achieving 100% success on synthetic planning tasks with zero LLM calls during inference vs 37 for LATS. *(arXiv)*
- **[KronQ: LLM quantization via Kronecker-factored Hessian](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07964)** — PTQ framework introducing gradient covariance into quantization. At 2-bit weight-only on LLaMA-3-70B achieves 7.93 perplexity while GPTQ and GPTAQ diverge. *(arXiv)*
- **[RIPO: Riemannian isometric policy optimization for LLM reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09559)** — Reveals PPO-Clip's Euclidean metric flaw and proposes isometric updates on the Riemannian manifold. Achieves up to 60% improvement over GRPO on AIME24 across seven benchmarks. *(arXiv)*
