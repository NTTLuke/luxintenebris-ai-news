## Lead Markets in turmoil: Chip stocks shed over $1 trillion as AI selloff deepens

## Top stories

### US bans foreign-made humanoid robots, FCC cites national security risks

The FCC banned imports of new foreign-made humanoid and quadruped robots, citing supply-chain vulnerabilities and cybersecurity risks to US critical infrastructure. Industry experts warn the move could hinder innovation while boosting domestic robotics.

Source: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/29/tech/us-china-robot-ban-intl-hnk)

### GPT-5.6 Sol self-optimization delivers 20% cost cut and 15% token efficiency gain

OpenAI announced GPT-5.6 Sol autonomously rewrote its own production GPU kernels and designed speculative decoding architectures, achieving 20% lower serving costs and 15%+ better token-generation efficiency through stack-wide optimizations.

Source: [OpenAI](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2082577277246972300)

### Moonshot AI surpasses funding goal to hit $35 billion valuation

China-based frontier AI lab and developer of the Kimi K3 model raised $3.5B in a pre-IPO round at a $35B valuation, underscoring the massive capital requirements of the frontier AI race.

Source: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/china-s-moonshot-ai-passes-funding-goal-to-hit-35-billion-value)

### ARC-AGI-3 score tripled with retained reasoning and context compaction

OpenAI showed that enabling retained reasoning and context compaction via the Responses API improved GPT-5.6 Sol's ARC-AGI-3 score by 188% while using 6x fewer output tokens, highlighting that benchmark scores heavily depend on harness and API settings.

Source: [OpenAI](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2082616636989952217)

## Research & Papers

- **[GPT-Red: automated red teaming via self-play at scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26115)** — OpenAI introduces an automated red-teaming agent trained to discover novel prompt injection attacks against frontier LLMs, used to adversarially train GPT-5.6. *(arXiv)*
- **[StealthBench: measuring operational stealth in autonomous offensive-security agents](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.26314)** — A new benchmark that evaluates whether autonomous offensive-security agents can achieve objectives without revealing their presence, capabilities, or collected intelligence. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Constitutional midtraining: content presence drives alignment gains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26654)** — Researchers test whether inserting principled values-based content during midtraining can produce durable alignment that doesn't erode under fine-tuning, showing midtraining interventions can outperform post-training alignment. *(arXiv)*
- **[HiFloat4: end-to-end FP4 reinforcement learning post-training for LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26515)** — The first end-to-end FP4 RL post-training where both rollout and training policies operate at 4-bit precision. A systematic study reveals the dominant source of degradation is not training-side quantization. *(arXiv)*
- **[SkillRise: agentic reinforcement learning for cross-task skill evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26784)** — A framework enabling LLM agents to accumulate and reuse skills across related tasks, treating skills as trainable states optimized through agentic reinforcement learning. *(arXiv)*
