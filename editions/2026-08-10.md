## Research & Papers

- **[Diffusion LLMs as targets and adversaries: mechanistic safety exploits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07430)** — Safety alignment in diffusion LLMs is sparse and transferable from autoregressive predecessors; self-pruning lifts attack success from 2.6% to 73.8% (LLaDA) and 1.9% to 86.6% (Dream), while the offline SN-Guided Diffusion jailbreak hits up to 77.1% transfer ASR on Llama-3-8B. *(arXiv)*
- **[Transformers struggle to use their emergent world models: revisiting Tower of Hanoi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07077)** — Small transformers develop a linearly-decodable Sierpinski-triangle world model, yet frontier models encode it near-perfectly at prompt end only to fail past three rings — a decay in representation maintenance, recoverable by re-injecting the prompt-time representation. *(arXiv)*
- **[Agent Memory Distillation: empowering small LLM agents with hierarchical teacher memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07169)** — A training-free framework transfers workflow, subtask and function memory from a large teacher to 4-8B student agents, gaining up to 27.2, 11.2 and 3.4 points on AppWorld, BFCL V3 and ToolSandbox with GPT-5-mini as teacher. *(arXiv)*
- **[SimWAM: a simple world action model for end-to-end autonomous driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07468)** — Uses video generation purely as a training signal via joint flow matching, discarding the video branch at inference to leave a self-contained action planner that reaches 91.5 PDMS on NAVSIM and transfers zero-shot to nuScenes. *(arXiv)*
- **[The Optimizer Is the Agent: reasoning-driven search across prompts, programs and ML workflows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06714)** — ReASearch internalizes an optimization search policy in a single tool-using agent, beating specialized optimizers by 2% to 40% across 14 tasks and sometimes surpassing prior human best-known results. *(arXiv)*
