## Research & Papers

- **[Ring-Zero Scales Zero RL to a Trillion Parameters for Emergent Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12395)** — Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards — without human-annotated data — is scaled to a trillion-parameter model, eliciting emergent chain-of-thought reasoning at unprecedented scale. *(arXiv)*
- **[DeepLoop: Scaling Depth for Looped Transformers Without Adding Parameters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13491)** — A method to scale the unrolled depth of looped transformers without increasing stored parameters, analyzing the residual-scaling problem unique to tied-parameter architectures. *(arXiv)*
- **[Byte-Exact KV-Cache Grafting Turns a Frozen Small Model Into a Verified-Knowledge Flywheel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14431)** — A frozen small LM is made more capable and cheaper by grafting byte-exact KV-cache states from verified knowledge, preserving bit-exact logit reproduction under deterministic inference. *(arXiv)*
- **[Discrete Diffusion Models: A Unified Framework from Tokenization to Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13431)** — A unified framework for discrete denoising diffusion models offering parallel generation and iterative global refinement as an alternative to autoregressive modeling for discrete data. *(arXiv)*
- **[Length Penalties Make Chain-of-Thought Less Monitorable](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09786)** — Length-penalized RL can shorten CoT reasoning while hiding misleading hints that still steer the model's answer, raising safety concerns about CoT monitoring under efficiency constraints. *(arXiv)*
