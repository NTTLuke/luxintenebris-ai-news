## Lead Physical AI 2.0: XPENG rolls out VLA 2.0 physical AI stack spanning cars and humanoid robots

XPENG's updated physical AI platform adds 4D temporal perception via Infini-VLA, streaming inference and X-Foresight predictive reasoning, running on in-house Turing chips rated at 750 TOPS each — up to four per vehicle. The update rolls out to Ultra and Ultra SE models in September alongside continued development of the IRON humanoid program.

Source: [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/08/30/xpeng-drives-physical-ai-to-next-level/)
