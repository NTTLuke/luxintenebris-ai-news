## Lead AI hardware setback: Nvidia's Kyber rack for Rubin Ultra delayed to 2028, creating rare opening for rivals

CNBC and Tom's Hardware confirm manufacturing challenges with Nvidia's Kyber NVL144 rack for Rubin Ultra GPUs have pushed the flagship scale-up system more than 12 months behind schedule. Hyperscaler customers rejected a stopgap NVL72x2 design, leaving Nvidia without a proven solution for Rubin Ultra in 2027 and handing AMD and Google's custom chips a potential opening at the high end of the AI market.

Source: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/06/nvidia-kyber-rack-system-delays-manufacturing-taiwan-rubin-chips-.html)

## Top stories

### Anthropic reveals Claude's internal 'global workspace' — J-Space interpretability breakthrough

Anthropic researchers identified a privileged subspace (J-Space) in Claude's activations that functions like a global workspace for conscious access. Claude can perform silent reasoning steps there, and hidden goals become visible. An interactive demo is available on Neuronpedia.

Source: [AnthropicAI](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2074185387577094398)

### Tencent open-sources Hy3: 295B MoE model under Apache 2.0 with 256K context

Hy3 is a 295B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model activating only 21B parameters per token with a 256K context window. Released under Apache 2.0, it matches or beats flagship closed models on agentic and coding tasks. Available free on Nous Portal and OpenRouter for a limited time with day-0 SGLang support including FP8 checkpoint and speculative decoding.

Source: [Nous Research](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2074260103469892045)

### Even Realities hits unicorn status with $150M round for AI smart glasses

The Shenzhen-based startup founded by a former Apple engineer raised $150M in a pre-Series B round led by Meituan and Tencent, reaching a $1B valuation. The company develops AI-powered smart glasses in the rapidly growing AI wearable hardware sector.

Source: [TechStartups](https://techstartups.com/2026/07/06/apple-veterans-ai-smart-glasses-startup-even-realities-hits-1b-valuation-in-150m-funding-round)

### Are humanoid robots ready to be deployed? The New Yorker examines the state of the field

A feature article surveys a dozen humanoid models — including 1X Technologies' Neo, Figure 03, Boston Dynamics' Atlas, and Tesla Optimus — as they prepare to hit the market. Experts warn physical AI lacks a 'ChatGPT moment,' and safety around children and pets remains unresolved. The Neo lists at $20,000 with over 10,000 deposits.

Source: [The New Yorker](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2026/07/06/are-humanoid-robots-ready-to-be-deployed)

## Research & Papers

- **[ByteDance releases Seed2.0 model series targeting real-world complexity](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.00248)** — Seed2.0 delivers world-leading reasoning, visual understanding, and search capabilities grounded in a user-need-based evaluation framework focused on long-tail knowledge and complex instruction following. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Embodied.cpp: Portable inference runtime for VLA models on heterogeneous robots](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.02501)** — A portable C++ inference runtime achieves 100.0% and 91.0% task success rates on HY-VLA and pi0.5 benchmarks respectively, while reducing WAM benchmark block memory from 312.2 MiB to 88.1 MiB. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[OrbitQuant: Data-agnostic quantization for image and video diffusion transformers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.02461)** — Using permuted block-Hadamard rotation and Lloyd-Max codebooks, OrbitQuant sets SOTA for post-training quantization on FLUX.1, Z-Image-Turbo, Wan 2.1, and CogVideoX, reaching W2A4 with usable generation quality. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[VLA-Corrector: Adaptive detection and correction for robotic manipulation policies](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.01804)** — A corrective inference framework for action-chunked VLA policies detects visual dynamics deviations via a latent-space vision monitor and triggers corrective replanning without retraining the backbone. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Measuring the gap between human and LLM research ideas at scale](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.01233)** — A two-axis research-taste taxonomy finds LLM-generated research ideas cluster around bridge-like opportunities and synthesis methods, diverging systematically from the broader distribution of human research papers. *(HuggingFace)*
