### Claude computes 9-loop scattering amplitude, beating prior human record

Anthropic's Science harness used Claude to compute a 9-loop six-particle scattering amplitude in planar N=4 super-Yang-Mills theory, surpassing the 2023 human record of 8 loops. The run cost a few thousand dollars and was largely unsupervised.

Source: [@marcopapa99](https://x.com/marcopapa99/status/2103779665911697779)

### Google will launch an AI data center satellite into orbit on October 1

The experimental satellite aboard a SpaceX Falcon 9 carries four TPUs and 1kW solar power to test AI inference in orbit, with plans for orbital data center fleets.
