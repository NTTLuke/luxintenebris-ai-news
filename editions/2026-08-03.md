## Lead Agentic Coding Demo: Karpathy turns a Lord of the Rings paragraph into a playable Three.js world with Opus 5

Karpathy handed Opus 5 a ~1M-token / ~$10 budget with the opening of The Lord of the Rings, and the model spent roughly two hours writing 5,500+ lines of Three.js to procedurally render and animate the story, self-correcting via screenshots. A playable browser version was published, spotlighting long-horizon agentic creative coding.

Source: [karpathy (X)](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2083749667410727319)

## Research & Papers

- **[WCM: A World Critic Model for Vision-Language-Action Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29613)** — Adds a future-state value estimator on top of VLA backbones to improve RL post-training for robotic manipulation, beating single-frame value-based baselines. *(arXiv)*
- **[The Parts Are Greater Than the Sum: automated task sequencing for multi-policy LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29601)** — Sequences heterogeneous tasks to reduce interference and catastrophic forgetting in shared low-rank adapter fine-tuning, improving transfer and retention over naive shared-LoRA. *(arXiv)*
- **[ExtractBench: a benchmark for schema-guided enterprise document extraction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29677)** — Evaluates agents on following a user-defined schema with source evidence grounding for enterprise document workflows. *(arXiv)*
- **[DungeonBench: rules-rich tactical reasoning in D&D combat](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29577)** — A benchmark where geometry, timing, resources and rule interactions all matter, turning decisions into measurable outcomes to stress-test models beyond typical game benchmarks. *(arXiv)*
- **[AMTFV: agentic mathematical tool-flow verification for LLM self-correction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29549)** — Verifies candidate answers via tool-flow rather than natural-language reflection only, improving answer verification and self-correction over reflection-only baselines. *(arXiv)*
