## Research & Papers

- **[RynnBrain 1.1: Embodied foundation model family achieves SOTA across multiple benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17977)** — Alibaba's RynnBrain 1.1 family (2B, 9B, 122B-A10B) uses a unified spatio-temporal framework. The 122B-A10B variant outperforms all proprietary and open-source models on VSI-Bench, MMSI, and RefSpatial-Bench. Real-robot experiments show RynnBrain-initialized policies outperform Qwen-based and generalist VLA baselines. *(arXiv)*
- **[Partially correlated verifier cascades: LLM reliability decays polynomially, not exponentially](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13918)** — A formal theory of correlated verifier cascades shows log-odds are concave in gate count, failure decays polynomially, and blind-spot atoms cap reliability. Independence-based extrapolation underestimates failure by 20x at k=5 and ~3000x at k=10. *(arXiv)*
- **[Agon introduces competitive cross-model RL with implicit rival grading](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07690)** — Two models grade each other's reasoning traces without process labels. On DeepMath with Qwen3, the approach doubles GRPO's pass@1. The method replicates across Qwen3.5 and Gemma 4 on competitive programming tasks. *(arXiv)*
- **[GEPO: Group entropy-controlled policy optimization extends GRPO for heterogeneous tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16850)** — A lightweight GRPO extension using group entropy for entropy-conditioned asymmetric advantage shaping. Consistently outperforms GRPO and recent entropy-controlled methods across 13 benchmarks spanning math, physics, code, and instruction following. *(arXiv)*
- **[RESOURCE2SKILL distills executable agent skills from multimodal human resources](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29538)** — Microsoft Research presents a framework that distills tutorials, repositories, and articles into executable skills for software agents, organized as a hierarchical Skill Wiki. Improves average score by +11.9 percentage points over no-skill agents. *(arXiv)*
