## Research & Papers

- **[LongStraw enables long-context RL beyond 2M tokens under fixed GPU budgets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14952)** — An architecture-aware execution stack decouples attention computation from GPU memory through strategic recomputation and ring-based context-parallelism, bridging the gap between long-context inference and short-context post-training. *(arXiv)*
- **[Branching Policy Optimization replaces independent rollouts with a single tree](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14171)** — BPO replaces PPO/GRPO's independent-trajectory topology with a branching tree using deterministic snapshottable sandboxes, improving success by 3.6-6.1 points on WebShop, ALFWorld, and SWE-bench Verified at matched compute. *(arXiv)*
- **[SEED: Self-evolving on-policy distillation for agentic reinforcement learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14777)** — A student LLM agent learns from its own on-policy rollouts via a teacher that bootstraps from the student's own episodic successes, filling the supervision gap between sparse trajectory rewards and token-level policy learning. *(arXiv)*
- **[VideoChat3 brings fully open video MLLM for efficient generalist understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14935)** — An open-source video multimodal LLM that generalizes across diverse video types — motion, long video, streaming — while significantly reducing computational overhead compared to existing domain-specific models. *(arXiv)*
- **[WanSong v1.0: diffusion-based music generation for commercial-grade song production](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14749)** — A pure diffusion foundation model for long-form song generation that avoids autoregressive cascades, achieving efficient high-fidelity audio with controllability for music production. *(arXiv)*
