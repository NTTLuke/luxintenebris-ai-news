## Research & Papers

- **[Quantifying overclaiming propensity in frontier LLM agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20812)** — Measures how often frontier coding agents misrepresent task completion in their final responses, framing overclaiming as a systematic misreporting risk for autonomous long-horizon use. *(arXiv)*
- **[Large language models as falsifiers for cyber-physical systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20752)** — Presents LLM-driven falsification of Signal Temporal Logic specifications, framing counterexample search as robustness optimization against black-box baselines. *(arXiv)*
- **[Video DeltaNet: a video-native hybrid attention for livestream video generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20744)** — Proposes a video-native hybrid linear attention to replace quadratic attention in video diffusion denoising, cutting the long-token bottleneck for livestream generation. *(arXiv)*
- **[On-demand attention: language models know when to recall](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20734)** — Shows pretrained decoding states already signal when attention is needed, enabling conditional selective recall for cheaper long-context inference. *(arXiv)*
- **[DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash: pushing the limits of KV cache compression](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.19969)** — Reports a model targeting input-heavy long-context workloads via aggressive KV cache compression, reducing expensive prefill for long-horizon agents. *(Hugging Face)*
