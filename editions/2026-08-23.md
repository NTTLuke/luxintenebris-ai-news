## Lead Humanoid sprint: Honor's Lightning humanoid runs 100m in 9.32s, beating Usain Bolt's world record

Chinese electronics maker Honor's self-developed Lightning humanoid clocked 100m in 9.32 seconds during a test ahead of the second World Humanoid Robot Games in Beijing, shaving 0.26s off Usain Bolt's 9.58s men's record and peaking at 14.5 m/s. The run underscores how quickly humanoid platforms are approaching — and now exceeding — human athletic performance.

Source: [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2026/aug/22/chinese-robot-runs-100m-sprint-quicker-usain-bolt-world-record)
