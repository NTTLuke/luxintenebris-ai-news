## Lead Deal of the year: NVIDIA buys Hugging Face for $12.9B, reshaping the open-model ecosystem

NVIDIA has agreed to acquire Hugging Face for about $12.9 billion, confirmed on September 3. The hub hosts more than 3 million models, 500,000 datasets and 1 million apps, making it the shared home of the open-weight AI world. The deal pushes NVIDIA from chips into the center of open-source AI and raises immediate questions about the platform's continued neutrality.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/nvidia-confirms-it-will-buy-hugging-face-for-12-9-billion/)

## Research & Papers

- **[Random Attention: rethinking KV cache eviction for efficient reasoning](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.03430)** — Randomly evicting KV cache entries within each head matches the strongest prior evictor across four models and six reasoning tasks while serving 32-43% higher throughput in vLLM, suggesting eviction selection signals add almost nothing. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[LLaDA-Image: open image generation with fully open training recipes](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.03796)** — A 6B diffusion transformer trained from scratch with a frozen diffusion-VLM module sets a new open-source state of the art on Qwen-Image-Bench and distills to 2-4 step Turbo inference. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[Terminal-Universe: turning agent trajectories into scalable terminal environments](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.04148)** — Researchers reconstruct 37,300 executable terminal environments from recorded agent trajectories; SFT of Qwen3.5-27B improves Terminal-Bench 2.1 by 11.9 points and EvoCode-Bench v2 by 13.8 points. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[It's the problem, not the path: budget and difficulty confounds in reasoning traces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03436)** — Restart-controlled truncation probes show that apparent 'breakthrough' moments and early-legible fates in LLM reasoning traces are confounded by test-time budget and problem difficulty, not by path properties. *(arXiv)*
- **[Declarative Attention slashes long-context compute with no fine-tuning](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2095612805496164801)** — A Google DeepMind paper lets models declare global, focus or local attention modes in chain-of-thought so the runtime skips KV cache reads, cutting attended tokens by 52% on Gemma-4-31B and 31% on Qwen-3.6-27B across 15 long-context tasks. *(Google DeepMind)*
