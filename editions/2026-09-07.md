## Lead Recursive Self-Improvement: OpenAI opens up its North Stars roadmap: automated research intern is live, automated researcher set for March 2028

OpenAI's transparency update says automated research interns now contribute about 3.1 workdays of runtime for every human workday inside its research organization. The next milestone, an automated AI researcher, is targeted for March 2028. The release includes an 'An Alien Mind' post and commentary from Noam Brown, Jakub Pachocki and Kevin Liu on keeping humans in the loop.

Source: [omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2096641382110707850)

## Top stories

### Hong Kong gets its first humanoid-staffed 24-hour convenience stores

Galbot's G1 humanoid Xiao Gai stocks shelves, handles checkout and serves customers in three Hong Kong outlets with no human staff, backed by the Hong Kong Investment Corporation. The pilot could expand to 100 stores across 10 cities.

Source: [@GalbotRobotics](https://x.com/GalbotRobotics/status/2096516636714942913)

### Google-backed Pixxel raises $100M in India's biggest space-tech round

A $100M Series C led by Temasek and Seraphim brings Pixxel's total funding to $195M for AI-driven satellite Earth-observation and its Aurora analytics platform.

Source: [India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/business/story/pixxel-raises-100-million-google-backed-indias-biggest-space-tech-funding-round-2988583-2026-09-07)

### AI data-center land deals hit $6B as rural America pushes back

US land purchases for AI data centers reached $6 billion in the first half of 2026, up 79% year over year, driving rural property prices to record highs while communities object over water, power and farmland loss.

Source: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/06/ai-data-centers-are-transforming-rural-land-markets-fueling-backlash.html)

### AI agents are starting to disrupt professional 3D modeling, says Simon Willison

Willison says artists using Blender with AI assistance are already working much faster, while roles that build models from others' designs face direct disruption, and asks whether cheaper animated production will expand the broader 3D asset market.

Source: [simonw](https://x.com/simonw/status/2096647662775017918)
