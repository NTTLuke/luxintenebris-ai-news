## Lead Frontier Release: Google unveils Gemini 4 Argon with a 1M-token output limit

Google DeepMind released Gemini 4 Argon, a new frontier model aimed at software engineering, enterprise knowledge work and defensive cybersecurity, with an industry-leading 1 million token output limit in a single pass — up from the previous 64K. The rollout is deliberately gated: it starts with trusted cyber-defense partners via the Fairwind Program before broader developer and consumer access, at an introductory price of $2/$10 per million input/output tokens.

Source: [Google DeepMind](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/)

### Anthropic: robots can do 74% of physical US job tasks — but stay uncompetitive

A new robot exposure index finds present-day robots can perform about 74% of physical US job tasks (34% of working hours) yet are cost-competitive for only 0.3% of them; robots plus LLMs expose roughly 80% of job tasks by working time.

Source: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/what-work-can-robots-do)

## Research & Papers

- **[RSIGame: autonomous agentic game development with recursive self-improvement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39045)** — An agentic game-dev framework with explore-diagnose-improve loops pushes Qwen3.8-27B past GPT-5.5 one-shot scores on GameCraft-Bench while cutting generation tokens 11x. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Scaling laws for looped mixture-of-experts models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40316)** — Derives joint scaling laws combining recurrence and sparsity — previously modeled only separately — quantifying how computational depth trades off against total capacity at fixed active compute. *(arXiv)*
- **[DC-SAE: a deep-compression semantic autoencoder for faster diffusion convergence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39222)** — A 32x spatial compression autoencoder with a pixel-level detail branch beats DC-AE by 13.5% PSNR and 54.9% gFID at 512x512 while accelerating diffusion training. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[WorldAuditBench: can agents audit interactive 3D worlds?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40325)** — A 213-task, 13-environment benchmark tests whether multimodal agents can couple navigation and visual reasoning to catch floating objects or traversable walls. Five frontier models score 6.6-42.3% versus 83.4% for humans. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[RoboCoach: world models as active coaches for compositional robot skills](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39685)** — Using imagined failures inside a shared world model to decide which demonstrations to collect lifts success from 13.3% to 75.0% on Franka and 40.0% to 83.8% on AgileX. *(HuggingFace)*
