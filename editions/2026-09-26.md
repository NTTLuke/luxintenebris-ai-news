## Lead AI Breaks Physics: Claude achieves 9-loop quantum physics calculation, solving long-standing challenge

Anthropic announced its Claude model computed the nine-loop correction to scattering amplitudes in planar N=4 super-Yang-Mills theory, a result previously considered unreachable with conventional methods. Physicist Lance Dixon independently verified the result, achieved for a few thousand dollars in compute — a fraction of the cost of traditional approaches.

Source: [AnthropicAI](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2103541577083719888)

California startup Feather Robotics emerged from stealth with a $29,990 bimanual mobile humanoid, backed by $7.6M from Gradient Ventures and already generating over $1M in revenue. Roughly half the price of Unitree's H2 Edu.

Source: [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/johnkoetsier/2026/09/25/feather-launches-29990-humanoid-robot-small-teams-are-rewriting-the-economics-of-humanoids/)

### Taste-Bench: new benchmark measures agent decision quality at fork points

Microsoft and collaborators released Taste-Bench, showing frontier models only achieve ~59.7% accuracy at picking the better path at decision forks. Distilling teacher judgment into student models improves downstream SWE-bench results.

Source: [omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2103478384584278502)
