## Lead Physical AI: Toyota unveils ELEY humanoid and plans a 400,000-unit factory deployment

Toyota revealed ELEY, a ~50 kg wheeled humanoid robot that learns tasks by observing veteran workers fitted with sensor jigs, mastering actions like fabric folding after roughly 1,500 repetitions. The automaker plans to invest about $6.4 billion a year to deploy 400,000 robots across its global production network beginning in 2028.

Source: [SpecsLib](https://specslib.com/en/news/toyota-eley-humanoid-deployment/)
