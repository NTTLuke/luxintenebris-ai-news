## Research & Papers

- **[RRC: unlocking generative reward models for LLM reinforcement learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06310)** — Identifies a mismatch between generative reward models and scalar scoring in existing RL, proposing Ranking-based Reward Construction to let generative reward models provide effective rewards for LLM policy optimization. *(arXiv)*
- **[Beyond Top-K: replacing black-box retrieval with interpretable agentic operations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06305)** — Argues chunk-embed-top-k retrieval is structurally unsound for table-dense documents — on a 780-page financial report, 86.8% of content lines were table rows — and replaces it with interpretable agentic retrieval operations. *(arXiv)*
- **[The Low Frequency Trap: video language models fail at simple event bookkeeping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06361)** — Introduces trace-grounded parametric profiling for event counting across 2,190 controlled video tasks, decoupling event count, rate, and duration to show video LMs systematically fail at basic temporal reasoning. *(arXiv)*
- **[GAUGE: a measurement-grounded benchmark for physical fidelity in simulation and world models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05948)** — A real-world-grounded diagnostic benchmark that jointly evaluates numerical physics simulators and generative video world models on physical fidelity, identifying which physical principles or parameters are violated. *(arXiv)*
- **[When History Lies: evaluating and improving tool use under misleading multi-turn histories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06057)** — Shows stale tool traces hijack agent policies — on Qwen3-1.7B, history pollution flips 32.1% of decisions that were correct under the original trajectory — and introduces a paired benchmark with synchronized Original, Polluted, and Oracle State views. *(arXiv)*
