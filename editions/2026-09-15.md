## Research & Papers

- **[Latent Interface Training breaks the vision-action shortcut for generalizable robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12641)** — Building a spatial-goal-conditioned action prior with no images and constraining visual conditioning through a pose-supervised latent interface lifts success across Pi0.5, MolmoAct2 and real-world unseen-camera tests. *(arXiv)*
- **[SAS sparsifies attention via end-to-end context ranking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13141)** — Simple Attention Sparsification trains an attention-sparse selector end-to-end inside a Triton FlashAttention kernel, beating trainable sparse-attention baselines on reasoning, long-context and agentic tasks. *(arXiv)*
- **[Feyospace-v1: how seven people trained frontier cyber models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08418)** — A data-centric post-training framework on 164K verified trajectories improves CyberGym by 23.76% with a small open-weight team, placing 10th on the official leaderboard. *(arXiv)*
- **[COBRA-Skills uses contextual bandits to optimize agent skills](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11682)** — Couples contextual-bandit prioritization with evidence-grounded skill evolution, cutting optimization cost 55-58% versus SkillOpt with only 50 examples per benchmark. *(arXiv)*
- **[Meta: byte-level models can beat token-based models at scale](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2099517031796347388)** — Byte-level models trail token-based ones at low compute but surpass them as scale grows, roughly 4% ahead at the ceiling and matching performance with as little as one-sixth the training data. *(omarsar0 (X))*
