### LLM verifiers emerge as a new scaling axis: continuous scoring without fine-tuning

A Stanford, NVIDIA, and UC Berkeley paper demonstrates extracting calibrated scores from LLM scoring-token logits, achieving 86.5% on Terminal-Bench V2 without fine-tuning, establishing verification as a new scaling axis alongside pre-training and test-time compute.

Source: [omarsar0 / Stanford, NVIDIA, UC Berkeley](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2074556579580711050)

### NVIDIA compresses hybrid MoE into Puzzle-75B-A9B, doubling inference throughput

NVIDIA's joint structural search compresses Nemotron-3-Super into Puzzle-75B-A9B, roughly doubling server throughput on 8xB200 nodes and improving H100 concurrency from 1 to 8 requests at 1M-token context.

Source: [omarsar0 / NVIDIA](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2074543978129793462)

## Research & Papers

- **[GigaWorld-1: roadmap to build world models for robot policy evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02642)** — A systematic study of 7 video world models with WMBench benchmark (324K+ simulated rollouts) finds evaluator quality is dominated by long-horizon action consistency, not short-term visual realism. *(arXiv)*
- **[EdgeBench: agent real-world learning follows log-sigmoid scaling laws](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05155)** — First evidence that agent real-world learning follows a log-sigmoid scaling law (R²=0.998) across 134 tasks and 38K hours of interaction. Agent learning speed doubles every 3 months. *(arXiv)*
- **[OmniOpt: comprehensive taxonomy and benchmarking of modern deep learning optimizers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04033)** — A 91-page survey taxonomy and geometric analysis of modern optimizers (Adam, Lion, Sophia, etc.) with standardized evaluation across multiple tasks. *(arXiv)*
- **[MANCE: manifold-aware concept erasure preserves unrelated information](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03973)** — Proposes the Manifold Constraint Hypothesis — constraining concept removal to the natural representation manifold better preserves unrelated data, achieving SOTA across 119 settings spanning text and vision models. *(arXiv)*
- **[KVpop: predictive online pruning for key-value cache compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05061)** — An online KV cache pruning method that predicts which attention keys and values to drop during LLM inference, reducing memory usage while maintaining generation quality. *(arXiv)*
