## Lead Humanoids, Mass-Produced: XPeng switches on the world's first automated humanoid robot production line

XPeng has started what it calls the world's first automated production line for advanced humanoid robots in Guangzhou, with more than 80% of core processes automated. Its IRON robot — 76 degrees of freedom and three in-house Turing AI chips rated at 2,250 TOPS — walked off the line, with mass production targeted for the end of 2026. The move lands as Tesla's Optimus program stalls.

Source: [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/09/07/xpeng-iron-humanoid-robot-production-line/)

## Top stories

### Nvidia chip export loophole clouds US-China AI summit talks

An investigation found Aivres, the unlisted US subsidiary of blacklisted Inspur, exported at least $5.6B in advanced tech to Southeast Asia between April 2024 and February 2026 — including over $3B in servers packed with Nvidia's most advanced Blackwell chips that ended up serving ByteDance and Alibaba. Lawmakers are pushing the Remote Access Security Act to close the cloud loophole.

Broadcom's fiscal Q3 2026 revenue hit $29.6B, with AI chip revenue up 221% to $16.7B. The company guided Q4 AI semiconductor revenue to $21.7B and full-year fiscal 2026 AI revenue to roughly $56-58B, with line of sight beyond $100B in FY2027.

## Research & Papers

- **[Verify before you distill: prompt-level teacher gating for on-policy distillation](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.02998)** — TGOPD verifies teacher reliability per prompt with small verifier-scored probe sets, then routes prompts to dense on-policy distillation or verifier-grounded GRPO. Across 4B and 35B students it beats vanilla OPD in all six single-domain settings and lifts teacher-node GPU utilization from 9.8% to 78.9%. *(Hugging Face)*
- **[What else needs fixing? Test-time compute for revision propagation in AI-generated artifacts](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.03254)** — A new benchmark tracks how local user revisions propagate through LLM-generated artifacts, evaluating nine revision methods with GPT-OSS, GPT-5.4-mini and Qwen3.5. Selecting from three parallel samples via LLM-based or medoid selection improves accuracy by 2.2-9.7% at the lowest cost. *(Hugging Face)*
- **['Design docs are all you need': DeepMind-led paper rebuilds code from docs](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2096983084956852537)** — A Google DeepMind and MIT study maintains a performance-modeling library as a graph of natural-language design docs; coding agents regenerate the implementation on every change, reproducing reference results to round-off precision. *(omarsar0)*
- **[DeepMind studies proactive agents that choose when to intervene](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2096780509540139364)** — A DeepMind study followed 16 writers for a week using agents with configurable roles and proactivity levels, finding users favor prospective planning and lightweight, non-directive presentation over intrusive interruptions. *(omarsar0)*
- **[NoRA: a one-line normalization tweak improves LoRA across training regimes](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2096837122892485069)** — NoRA normalizes the LoRA down-projection during training or at initialization, improving convergence, stability and final performance with zero extra parameters or inference cost across pretraining, SFT and RL. *(omarsar0)*

## Hardware & Robotics

- **[Xiaomi's L3 autonomous driving lead exits to launch a physical AI startup](https://cnevpost.com/2026/09/08/xiaomi-l3-lead-exits-for-physical-ai/)** — Wang Naiyan, former head of Xiaomi Auto's L3 autonomous driving technology, has left to start a physical AI venture, joining a wave of Chinese automotive talent moving into embodied AI as Xiaomi prepares its own electric car for a robotaxi trial. *(CnEVPost)*
- **[AI data centers are less thirsty now, tech giants say](https://techxplore.com/news/2026-09-ai-centers-thirsty-tech-giants.html)** — Data-center water consumption reached 222 billion liters globally in 2025 and could nearly triple by 2030, but closed-loop liquid cooling is improving efficiency: Nvidia says its DSX design nearly eliminates water use with 45°C coolant, while Microsoft and AWS improved water-use efficiency 25% and 37% between 2022 and 2025. *(Tech Xplore)*
- **[Robot learns to walk like a stick insect using AI](https://newatlas.com/ai-humanoids/teaching-hexapedal-robot-walk-stick-insect/)** — Researchers from Tohoku University and VISTEC used adversarial inverse reinforcement learning to teach a hexapedal robot stick-insect style locomotion from brief observation, mastering varied terrain in under an hour with a reward network that transfers across body types. *(New Atlas)*
- **[MassRobotics survey finds industry split on FCC robot restrictions](https://www.therobotreport.com/massrobotics-shares-member-survey-results-around-fcc-restrictions/)** — A MassRobotics survey of member companies found the industry evenly split on the FCC's July 2026 decision adding foreign-produced advanced robotics devices — including some mobile robots and humanoids — to its Covered List, with views largely tracking where companies manufacture and source components. *(The Robot Report)*
- **[Inbolt: physical AI's factory bottleneck is deployment, not training data](https://www.therobotreport.com/inbolt-ceo-to-discuss-physical-ais-deployment-problem-at-robobusiness/)** — After deploying vision-guided control across over 100 factories and 40M-plus robot cycles, Inbolt's CEO argues physical AI progress in the factory is gated by deployment and part-handling constraints rather than raw training data, as in-hand localization lets robots adjust trajectories mid-move. *(The Robot Report)*
