## Lead Voice-powered frontier models: OpenAI launches GPT-6 Astra, Sol and Luna for ChatGPT Voice with ChatGPT Work mode

OpenAI rolled out three GPT-6 variants powering ChatGPT Voice: Astra (general-purpose), Sol (reasoning-heavy), and Luna (cost-efficient). All support native plugin and tool use, plus a new ChatGPT Work mode enabling voice-driven document, slide, and spreadsheet creation. The release marks the first time GPT-6 models are available through the voice interface at full reasoning capability.

## Research & Papers

- **[Decomposed subtask RL beats vanilla GRPO by 11-24% on AgentBench and WebArena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27035)** — DSRL segments rollouts into subtask-level segments and assigns rewards separately, achieving 3-5x faster convergence over monolithic credit assignment. *(arXiv)*
- **[Agent-Editing World Model cuts LLM agent token cost by 2.5x while boosting success rate](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28416)** — AWM edits the agent's internal belief state via a lightweight diff model instead of reconstructing full observations, achieving 28% higher task success on ALFWorld and WebArena. *(arXiv)*
- **[On-policy distillation unifies teacher and student distributions for low-bit reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26708)** — OPD recovers 94% of CodeContests and 96% of GSM8K accuracy at 2-bit weights versus 68% and 72% for offline quantization-aware distillation. *(arXiv)*
- **[PACT aligns critic credit estimates with token-level outcomes, matching GRPO with 40% fewer steps](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.26355)** — Learns a dense reward model satisfying completeness, locality, and eligibility, with 2x higher correlation to held-out token-level outcomes. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[SpeakerMem-R1 achieves 84% accuracy on multi-party dialogue memory benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26780)** — Dual-track memory separating personal narratives from shared group knowledge outperforms existing multi-party memory systems by 18%. *(arXiv)*
