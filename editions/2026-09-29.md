## Research & Papers

- **[Frontier Learning: training LLM reasoners at the edge of capability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35426)** — Proposes training LLM reasoners on problems at the edge of their capability rather than a fixed pool, using GRPO with adaptive difficulty selection to improve reasoning generalization. *(arXiv)*
- **[How to Loop MoE: flatten the experts, untie the attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35751)** — Bridges looped Transformers (reusing one block multiple times) with sparse mixture-of-experts to push fixed-size models further while activating only a few experts per token. *(arXiv)*
- **[Distillation defenses easily break after reinforcement learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35699)** — Demonstrates that existing distillation defenses against model stealing are easily circumvented when the attacker applies RL to the distilled model, recovering frontier-level performance. *(arXiv)*
- **[VEX-Bench: benchmarking verification complexity of LLM-generated misinformation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35028)** — Benchmark measuring the verification difficulty of LLM-generated misinformation, quantifying the asymmetry between cheap content production and expensive fact-checking. *(arXiv)*
- **[Behavioral foundation models for quality diversity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35615)** — Proposes Behavioral Foundation Models (BFMs) for RL, analogous to LLMs in NLP, demonstrating zero-shot performance, fast imitation and online adaptation from diverse behavior trajectories. *(arXiv)*
