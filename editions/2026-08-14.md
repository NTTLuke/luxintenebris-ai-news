## Research & Papers

- **[Reduced Matrix Multiplication: training-free LLM inference speedup](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13426)** — RMM selectively reduces Transformer matrix products by identifying and skipping redundant computations, offering a training-free path to faster inference without quality loss. *(arXiv)*
- **[Step-level self-distilled policy optimization for deep search agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12764)** — OPSD addresses sparse reward in multi-step search trajectories by using the model's own logits for step-level credit assignment, enabling more efficient RL for deep search agents. *(arXiv)*
- **[How do VLMs behave when blind or misled?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13267)** — A behavioral evaluation of vision-language models under uncertainty, finding that models can internally distinguish when abstention is required but often fail to express it. *(arXiv)*
- **[Spatial Memory Agent: experience-grounded memory for spatial intelligence](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.12743)** — A framework that gives VLM agents procedural memory for spatial reasoning, improving performance on embodied tasks, robotic planning, and multimodal assistants. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[Training AI scientists to replicate research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13331)** — A paper exploring how to train AI systems to independently replicate scientific results, illuminating underspecified details and strengthening the reproducibility pipeline. *(arXiv)*
