## Research & Papers

- **[CoTrace: data recipes for training terminal agents with harness-model co-evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10426)** — Terminal-agent capability depends jointly on model weights and the runtime harness. CoTrace introduces data recipes that treat trajectories produced during harness search as differentiated training signal. *(arXiv)*
- **[What matters for latent reasoning with flow matching](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.06666)** — Identifies training choices for flow matching in latent reasoning space. The FLaRe recipe reaches 97% of explicit CoT accuracy at 25% latency, evaluated across five requirements. *(HuggingFace)*
- **[EngramEdit: decoupled knowledge updates in LLMs through conditional memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10533)** — Uses the DeepSeek Engram architecture to decouple factual knowledge storage from model parameters, enabling targeted edits without full retraining. *(arXiv)*
- **[YANchor-4B: effective long-horizon reasoning in O(N) time with O(1) memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10118)** — A general-purpose recurrent model that preserves crucial information across long horizons while keeping generation cost manageable. Achieves strong results at 4B scale. *(arXiv)*
- **[SemanticFold: latent sequence compression separates language modeling, decodability, and reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10304)** — Compresses prefix hidden states at learned boundaries, evaluated across five model scales from Qwen3-1.7B to 27B. *(arXiv)*
