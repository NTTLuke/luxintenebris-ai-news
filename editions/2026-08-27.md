## Research & Papers

- **[VBVR-Pro: a verifiable suite for native visual reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26105)** — A closed-loop testbed of 300 procedurally generated tasks whose deterministic reward scorers outperform the VLM-as-a-judge paradigm; models trained on it transfer across seven external visual reasoning benchmarks. *(arXiv)*
- **[Spectral Allocation: why Muon beats Adam, and how to improve Muon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25990)** — Out-of-sample spectral probing shows Transformer loss landscapes are anisotropic and stable; SAMuon with a static spectral prior needs 13.3-24.0% fewer training tokens to match Muon on 124M-1B parameter models. *(arXiv)*
- **[R^3: training robots to reason in natural language via RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26053)** — A post-training recipe of mid-training on expert reasoning traces plus single-step rubric-based RL turns off-the-shelf VLMs into robotic reasoners, beating instruction-only imitation baselines. *(arXiv)*
- **[AsymSpec: context-asymmetric speculative decoding for agentic LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26004)** — A lightweight drafter reads full context while a large verifier works on a compressed view, reaching ~90% of full-context accuracy at 1.3-1.7x throughput and 0.2-0.3x compute cost. *(arXiv)*
- **[VISA: agentic self-evolving data synthesis for multimodal instruction following](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26013)** — A self-evolving loop that reuses failure feedback and verifier outcomes across rounds, improving MM-IFEval over strong baselines while its verifier contracts double as RL rewards. *(arXiv)*
