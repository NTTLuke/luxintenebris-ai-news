SemaPLC: verification-gated PLC code generation

A project-grounded, verification-gated agent harness that only declares tasks complete when external checks pass. Record 72.6% mean strict verified pass rate across seven models and a dynamic-behavior score of 52.2 vs 22.4-31.4 for baselines on live PLC runtime traces.

SPADE: self-play in adaptive synthetic executable environments

One LLM writes executable Gym-style environments while another solves them. Scaling to 30B parameters improves over the strongest fixed-environment baseline by +5.3 on average across eight held-out benchmarks, and lifts tool-use by +5.7 on BFCL-v4 and +13.9 on ACEBench-Agent.

LLMs run a football club for 20 in-game years with ~340-400 decision stops and 26 tools; all 15 frontier models finish where scripted baselines die, and claude-fable-5 tops both solo and Arena tracks with the title rotating among ten models.

SkillGate: training in-policy skill selection for long-horizon agents

Giving skill-naming tokens an action-local advantage in a 16-candidate slate lifts a 9B policy from 40.8% to 53.2% trial success across five agentic benchmarks, while cutting misleading skill exposure by two thirds.

Study: agents lock into post-training strategies early

Examining a large set of public post-training trajectories, researchers find agents fix their initial training strategy early and then only adjust locally. Experience-driven scaffolds gave +12.6 on GSM8K and +40.8 on HumanEval, while extra inference compute did little to enable mid-execution shifts.
