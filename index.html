Single-rollout asynchronous optimization beats GRPO for LLM post-training

SAO replaces group-wise sampling in GRPO with single-rollout sampling and strict double-side token-level clipping to stabilize asynchronous RL. Consistently outperforms GRPO on SWE-Bench Verified, BeyondAIME, and IMOAnswerBench. Deployed in GLM-5.2's agentic RL pipeline.

Sparse Delta Memory scales linear RNN capacity through sparse reads and writes

SDM extends Gated DeltaNet with sparse reads and writes to a large explicit memory, scaling hidden state capacity by orders of magnitude under isoFLOP constraint. Improves in-context learning and long-context retrieval over dense linear RNNs.
